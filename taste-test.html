Taste Test

Can you guess the mystery dish?

How to play

  • 1 Guess the mystery dish in up to 5 attempts. All answers are single-word dishes, exactly 5 letters long.
  • 2 After each guess, the letter tiles reveal how close you were:

    R Green — right letter, right position A Yellow — right letter, wrong position X Grey — letter not in the dish
  • 3 A new hint unlocks after each wrong guess:
Miss 1 Dish type (Sweet / Savoury / Spicy)
Miss 2 Country of origin
Miss 3 Description of the dish
Miss 5 The answer is revealed

✦ All guesses must be dishes from the Taste Test game database. If a dish isn't recognised, try a different one.
